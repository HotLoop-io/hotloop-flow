HotLoop Flow 2.17.2 brings 2.17.1 to arm64. One image now covers your x86 boxes and your arm64 ones, like a 64-bit Raspberry Pi or an ARM edge gateway. There is no change to the code, the chart, the settings or your flows.

## What changed

- The image is built for linux/amd64 and linux/arm64. The `2.17.2`, `2.17` and `2` tags hold both.
- The README and the install pages name 2.17.2 and say amd64 and arm64.

## New settings

None.

## Upgrade

A plain `helm upgrade` to the 2.17.2 image and chart, or the new tag in your Quadlet unit. The chart takes its image tag from its own version, so an arm64 box on a 2.17.1 chart install moves to 2.17.2 to get its image. If you are on arm64 and stayed on 2.16.1, this is your move; read the 2.17.1 notes for everything new since. Nothing happens on first start and nothing needs migrating.

Image: `ghcr.io/hotloop-io/hotloop-flow:2.17.2`, linux/amd64 and linux/arm64. Chart: `https://hotloop.io/hotloop-flow/`. The README at this tag has the rest, with pictures: https://github.com/HotLoop-io/hotloop-flow/blob/v2.17.2/README.md
